\textbf{Research Title:} Leveraging Machine Learning for Data-Driven and Personalised Voice Bundle Recommendations: Improving Customer Satisfaction and Informing Business Decisions for Revenue Optimisation. A Case Study of Airtel Uganda Limited.

\textbf{Interviewer:} Remmy Bisimbeko

\vspace{0.3cm}
\noindent
Interviewee: \underline{\hspace{6cm}} Title: \underline{\hspace{4cm}} \\[0.4cm]
Date: \underline{\hspace{4cm}} Time: \underline{\hspace{4cm}} \\[0.2cm]
Duration: 45--60 minutes

\vspace{0.4cm}
\textbf{Section I: Introduction and Consent (5 minutes)}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Introduce yourself and the purpose of the study.
    \item Explain confidentiality: responses will be anonymised and aggregated in the final report. The focus is on processes and strategy, not individual performance.
    \item Obtain written consent. Confirm whether audio recording is acceptable.
    \item Confirm the estimated duration and the participant's right to withdraw or decline any question at any time.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{Section II: Current State of Recommendations}

\textit{Goal: To assess the existing recommendation processes, their limitations, and key business pain points.}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Current System:} How are voice bundle promotions and recommendations currently generated and delivered to the customer base? \textit{Can you describe the main decision-making process (manual vs.\ automated rules)?}

    \item \textbf{Criteria \& Targeting:} What are the key business rules or metrics used to decide which customers receive which bundle recommendations? \textit{Are these rules primarily based on ARPU segmentation, historical spend, or general demographics?}

    \item \textbf{Challenges:} What are the major challenges the marketing or product team currently faces in ensuring customers buy the most optimal bundle? \textit{What is the typical conversion rate, and what percentage of the budget is considered `wasted' on untargeted messages?}

    \item \textbf{Success Metrics:} How does Airtel currently measure the success or effectiveness of a recommendation campaign? \textit{Are you satisfied with the level of detail available in these metrics, particularly regarding customer satisfaction?}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{Section III: Data and Technical Feasibility}

\textit{Goal: To understand the data environment, feature value, and technical readiness for a machine learning implementation.}

\begin{enumerate}
    \setcounter{enumi}{4}
    \item \textbf{Key Data Features:} From a data perspective, which customer attributes do you believe are the strongest predictors of voice bundle purchasing behaviour? \textit{Specifically ask about real-time metrics like depletion rate vs.\ long-term metrics like tenure/ARPU.}

    \item \textbf{Data Access / Quality:} How is customer usage data typically stored and accessed for analytical purposes? \textit{What are the main data governance or privacy considerations for using customer data in this way?}

    \item \textbf{ML Readiness:} What machine learning or analytical tools are currently used by the data science or IT team? \textit{Is there existing infrastructure or a platform that could support a new recommendation engine requiring real-time triggering?}

    \item \textbf{Implementation:} If an ML-based system were implemented, what specific technical requirements would be critical for deployment? \textit{How would the output of the model integrate with the existing USSD/SMS/App delivery channels?}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{Section IV: Business Impact and Future Strategy}

\textit{Goal: To evaluate the perceived impact on customer satisfaction, revenue, and strategic decision-making.}

\begin{enumerate}
    \setcounter{enumi}{8}
    \item \textbf{Revenue Optimisation:} How important is personalised recommendation in driving ARPU and reducing revenue leakage from sub-optimal purchases? \textit{If a system could increase the probability of a customer buying a higher-value bundle, what would that mean for the business?}

    \item \textbf{Customer Satisfaction:} In your opinion, how would highly accurate, personalised voice bundle recommendations impact customer satisfaction and churn rates? \textit{Do you believe customers are currently frustrated by irrelevant offers, and how big a problem is this?}

    \item \textbf{Strategic Decision-Making:} Beyond immediate recommendations, how could the insights gained from an ML model inform broader product development or pricing strategies? \textit{Could the model help identify underserved customer segments or gaps in the current bundle portfolio?}

    \item \textbf{Risk \& Buy-in:} What potential business risks do you foresee in implementing a fully automated, ML-driven recommendation system? \textit{What level of internal buy-in (from leadership, IT, marketing) would be needed for a project like this?}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{Section V: Conclusion (5 minutes)}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Summarise two to three key takeaways from the discussion and confirm accuracy with the participant.
    \item Advise that findings will be used to contextualise model results and ensure the system design meets real-world business needs. A follow-up for clarification may be requested.
    \item Ask whether the participant has any final questions or points to add.
    \item Thank the participant for their time and contribution.
\end{enumerate}
