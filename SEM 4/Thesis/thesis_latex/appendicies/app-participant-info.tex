\textbf{Study Title:} Leveraging Machine Learning for Data-Driven and Personalised Voice Bundle Recommendations: Improving Customer Satisfaction and Informing Business Decisions for Revenue Optimisation. A Case Study of Airtel Uganda Limited.

\textbf{Researcher:} Bisimbeko Remmy | J24MD19/007 | MSc Data Science and Analytics

\textbf{Supervisor:} Dr.\ Daphne Nyachaki Bitalo

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{PURPOSE OF THE STUDY}

You are being invited to participate in a research study that aims to develop a machine learning-based personalised voice bundle recommendation system for Airtel Uganda Limited. The study seeks to understand the current state of customer recommendations at Airtel Uganda, identify the limitations of existing approaches, and assess the feasibility of a data-driven recommendation framework.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{WHY YOU HAVE BEEN INVITED}

You have been selected because of your professional role within Airtel Uganda's commercial, marketing, data analytics, or technology functions, and your knowledge of how customer recommendations are currently developed and delivered.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{WHAT PARTICIPATION INVOLVES}

Participation involves a single semi-structured interview of approximately 45 to 60 minutes. The interview will cover the following topics:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The current state of voice bundle recommendation processes at Airtel Uganda
    \item The data and technical infrastructure available to support machine learning implementations
    \item The perceived business impact of personalised recommendations on customer satisfaction and revenue
    \item Strategic considerations for deploying an ML-based recommendation engine
\end{itemize}

With your consent, the interview may be audio-recorded to ensure accurate transcription. You are free to decline recording, in which case written notes will be taken instead.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION AND WITHDRAWAL}

Participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You have the right to decline any question and to withdraw from the study at any time without consequence or penalty. Withdrawal will not affect your employment or standing at Airtel Uganda Limited.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{CONFIDENTIALITY AND ANONYMITY}

All responses will be treated as strictly confidential. Your name, title, and identifying information will not appear in any published research output. Responses will be anonymised and aggregated, and no individual participant will be identifiable in the thesis or any resulting publications. Audio recordings will be deleted following transcription and verification.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{USE OF FINDINGS}

The findings from this study will be used to contextualise the quantitative analytical results and to ensure that the proposed recommendation framework is aligned with Airtel Uganda's real-world operational context. The completed thesis will be submitted to Uganda Christian University and may be made publicly available through the UCU library.
