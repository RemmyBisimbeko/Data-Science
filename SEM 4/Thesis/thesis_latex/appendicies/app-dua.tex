\textbf{2. PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT}

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms and conditions under which Airtel Uganda Limited will provide the Researcher with access to anonymised customer transactional data for the sole purpose of conducting academic research in partial fulfilment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Science in Data Science and Analytics at Uganda Christian University.

The research is titled: \textit{``Leveraging Machine Learning for Data-Driven and Personalised Voice Bundle Recommendations: Improving Customer Satisfaction and Informing Business Decisions for Revenue Optimisation. A Case Study of Airtel Uganda Limited.''}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{3. DESCRIPTION OF THE DATASET}

The Data Provider agrees to provide the Researcher with access to the following anonymised dataset:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Voice bundle purchase transaction records covering a 90-day observation period (February to April 2026), comprising records from 1,458,900 unique active prepaid subscribers across the Kampala, Central 1, and Central 2 regions.
    \item The dataset includes: bundle type purchased, purchase price, timestamp of each transaction, Call Detail Records (CDRs) summarising call frequency and duration per subscriber, usage volume data, and anonymised customer demographic and subscription information, including geographic region and customer tenure.
    \item All personally identifiable information --- including but not limited to mobile subscriber numbers, names, and national identification details --- was replaced with unique, non-identifiable identifiers prior to analysis. No data capable of identifying any individual subscriber is included in the research outputs.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{4. PERMITTED USE OF THE DATA}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The Researcher may use the dataset solely for the academic research purposes described in Section 2 of this Agreement.
    \item Permitted activities include: exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, machine learning model training and evaluation, and the generation of aggregated statistical findings for inclusion in the academic thesis.
    \item The Researcher may include aggregated, non-identifiable summary statistics, charts, and model performance metrics derived from the dataset in the thesis and any resulting academic publications.
    \item The Researcher shall not share, sell, transfer, or disclose the raw dataset to any third party without prior written consent from the Data Provider.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{5. DATA SECURITY AND HANDLING}

The Researcher shall store all data on encrypted, password-protected personal devices accessible only to the Researcher and immediate academic supervisor. All raw data files will be deleted from personal devices within six (6) months of thesis submission.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY}

All intellectual property in the raw dataset remains with Airtel Uganda Limited. The analytical findings, models, and written outputs of the research are the intellectual property of the Researcher and Uganda Christian University.
