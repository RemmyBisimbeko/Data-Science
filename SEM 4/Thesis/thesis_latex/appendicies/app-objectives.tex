\textbf{Main Objective}

The main objective of this study is to identify the key customer behavioural attributes that can be used to predict appropriate voice bundles for individual Airtel Uganda subscribers, and to leverage these attributes to develop a machine learning-based recommendation framework that supports Airtel Uganda's business growth objectives.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{Specific Objectives}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item To identify the key customer behavioural attributes and patterns that are most predictive of voice bundle purchase decisions among Airtel Uganda prepaid subscribers.

    \item To evaluate the projected business impact of the best-performing machine learning model on voice bundle offer conversion rate and Average Revenue Per User, and to formulate evidence-based strategic recommendations for Airtel Uganda to improve customer satisfaction and optimise marketing efficiency.
    
    \item To formulate strategic, evidence-based machine learning recommendations for Airtel Uganda to grow its business and improve customer satisfaction.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{Research Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item What are the key customer behavioural attributes and patterns that most effectively predict voice bundle purchase decisions among Airtel Uganda prepaid subscribers?

    \item Which machine learning model most accurately predicts the appropriate voice bundle for a given Airtel Uganda customer?

    \item How can the outputs of a data-driven machine learning recommendation model inform strategic business decisions for Airtel Uganda in order to improve customer satisfaction and optimise revenue?
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{Research Hypotheses}

\textbf{Hypothesis 1 (H1):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item H1\textsubscript{0}: No customer behavioural attribute is significantly associated with the type of voice bundle a subscriber is most likely to purchase.
    \item H1\textsubscript{1}: At least one customer behavioural attribute is a significant predictor of the voice bundle a subscriber is most likely to purchase.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Hypothesis 2 (H2):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item H2\textsubscript{0}: There is no significant difference in predictive accuracy between the machine learning models evaluated for the voice bundle classification task.
    \item H2\textsubscript{1}: At least one machine learning model significantly outperforms the others in predictive accuracy, as measured by Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1-Score.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Hypothesis 3 (H3):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item H3\textsubscript{0}: The best-performing machine learning model does not generate recommendations that project a statistically meaningful improvement in voice bundle offer conversion rate above the current baseline of approximately 3 percent, as estimated from the model's weighted precision on the hold-out test set.
    \item H3\textsubscript{1}: The best-performing machine learning model generates recommendations that project a statistically meaningful improvement in voice bundle offer conversion rate above the current baseline of approximately 3 percent, as estimated from the model's weighted precision on the hold-out test set and benchmarked against documented industry outcomes (McKinsey \& Company, 2022; 2024) for Airtel Uganda.
\end{itemize}
